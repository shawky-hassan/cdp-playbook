What CDP actually is

Simple view

CDP is the layer between your source systems and your activation tools.

  • Ingests from CRM, Marketo, Sales Cloud, Outreach, MDG.
  • Resolves duplicate identities into one unified customer profile.
  • Sends segments and triggers downstream.
Why it matters to you

One trusted audience layer instead of working separately in each tool.

  • Marketo becomes the execution engine, not the system of truth.
  • Salesforce provides CRM facts and opportunity context.
  • Demandbase becomes an intelligence input layer.

The core CDP flow

Collect → unify → segment → activate.

Exploration

Drag-and-drop builder where you define audience logic.

Audience

Scheduled or on-demand batch output pushed to Marketo or S3.

CX Flow

Real-time automation triggered by profile updates or indicator changes.

The objects that matter most

Four concepts needed before building any exploration.

Unified Customer Profile

The golden-record view used for segmentation after merging all source contributors.

Contextual Profile

The source-level contributor view — individual records from Salesforce, Marketo, etc.

Indicators

The KPI engine — activity, calculated, predictive indicators. Use these, not raw activity counts.

Segments

Classification layer — Gold / Silver / Platinum or Low / Medium / High — based on indicator thresholds.
