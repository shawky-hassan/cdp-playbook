What CDP actually is

The layer between your source systems and activation tools.

Simple view

The layer between source systems and activation tools.

  • ›Ingests from CRM, Marketo, Sales Cloud, Outreach, MDG.
  • ›Resolves duplicates into one unified customer profile.
  • ›Sends segments and triggers downstream.
Why it matters

One trusted audience layer instead of working separately in each tool.

  • ›Marketo becomes execution engine, not system of truth.
  • ›Salesforce provides CRM facts and opportunity context.
  • ›Demandbase becomes an intelligence input layer.

The core CDP flow

Collect → unify → segment → activate.

Exploration

Drag-and-drop builder where you define audience logic before publishing.

Audience

Scheduled or on-demand batch output pushed to Marketo or S3.

CX Flow

Real-time automation triggered by profile updates or indicator changes.

The objects that matter most

Four concepts needed before building any exploration.

Unified Customer Profile

Golden-record view used for segmentation after merging all source contributors.

Contextual Profile

Source-level contributor view — individual records from Salesforce, Marketo, etc.

Indicators

The KPI engine — activity, calculated, predictive. Use these, not raw activity counts.

Segments

Classification layer — Gold / Silver / Platinum — based on indicator thresholds.

Rule: Always use pre-built calculated indicators in explorations, not raw activity counts.
